The extension watches network requests in your browser and builds your tracker cloud in real time. It takes about 30 seconds to set up.

Download Extension

Works on Edge, Chrome and any Chromium browser · ~50KB

1
Download and unzip
Click the button above and unzip the downloaded file to a folder on your computer.
2
Open your browser extensions
In Edge: go to edge://extensions
In Chrome: go to chrome://extensions
3
Enable Developer mode
Toggle the Developer mode switch in the top right corner of the extensions page.
4
Load the extension
Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder. The FreeFollowers icon will appear in your toolbar.
5
Browse any website
Visit a few websites, then click the FreeFollowers icon in your toolbar — your tracker cloud opens automatically.
